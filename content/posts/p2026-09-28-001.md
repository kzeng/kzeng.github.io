# 书法学习隐私政策

  ## Privacy Policy for 书法学习

  生效日期 / Effective Date：2026 年 9 月 28 日

  感谢您使用“书法学习”（以下简称“本应用”）。我们尊重并保护您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何访问、使用和处理相关信息。

  Thank you for using “书法学习” (“the App”). We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the App accesses, uses, and handles information.

  ## 1. 应用基本情况 / About the App

  本应用是一款用于浏览和学习书法碑帖的 Android 应用。

  本应用：

  - 不要求用户注册或登录；
  - 不提供社交、评论或聊天功能；
  - 不包含广告；
  - 不提供支付或订阅功能；
  - 不收集位置、通讯录、相机、麦克风、短信或通话记录；
  - 不建立自有用户账号系统。

  The App is an Android application for browsing and learning Chinese calligraphy inscriptions.

  The App:

  - Does not require registration or login;
  - Does not provide social, comment, or chat features;
  - Does not contain advertisements;
  - Does not provide payment or subscription features;
  - Does not collect location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or call data;
  - Does not operate its own user account system.

  ## 2. 本地保存的数据 / Data Stored Locally

  本应用可能在您的设备本地保存以下数据：

  - 收藏的字帖；
  - 显示主题设置；
  - 字体大小设置；
  - 用户添加的字帖信息；
  - 应用运行所需的本地数据库和缓存数据。

  这些数据主要保存在您的 Android 设备本地，不会上传到本应用自有服务器。

  The App may store the following data locally on your device:

  - Favorite calligraphy posts;
  - Display theme settings;
  - Font size settings;
  - Calligraphy post information added by the user;
  - Local database and cache data required for the App to function.

  This data is primarily stored locally on your Android device and is not uploaded to servers operated by us.

  您可以通过 Android 系统设置清除本应用数据，或卸载本应用，以删除设备上的相关本地数据。

  You may delete locally stored data by clearing the App’s data in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

  ## 3. 网络访问和第三方服务 / Network Access and Third-Party Services

  本应用需要访问互联网，以加载字帖图片、搜索结果和其他字帖内容。

  The App accesses the internet to load calligraphy images, search results, and other calligraphy content.

  当您使用搜索功能时，您输入的搜索关键词可能会发送至第三方网站，例如：

  - https://www.zitiewang.com/
  - 字帖数据中记录的其他第三方图片或内容网站。

  When you use the search feature, the search keywords you enter may be sent to third-party websites, including:

  - https://www.zitiewang.com/
  - Other third-party image or content websites referenced by the calligraphy data.

  第三方网站可能根据其自身隐私政策处理网络请求信息，例如 IP 地址、请求时间、设备信息和访问日志。本应用不控制第三方网站的数据处理行为。

  Third-party websites may process network request information, such as IP address, request time, device information, and access logs, according to their own privacy policies. We do not control the data
  practices of these third-party websites.

  请不要在搜索框中输入姓名、电话号码、电子邮箱、住址或其他不必要的个人信息。

  Please do not enter your name, phone number, email address, home address, or other unnecessary personal information into the search field.

  ## 4. 数据收集、使用和分享 / Collection, Use, and Sharing of Data

  本应用不建立自有用户账号，也不会出售、出租或向第三方营销机构提供用户个人信息。

  The App does not operate its own user account system and does not sell, rent, or provide users’ personal information to marketing companies.

  本应用不会主动将收藏、主题设置、字体大小或用户添加的字帖上传到本应用自有服务器。

  The App does not actively upload favorites, theme settings, font size settings, or user-added calligraphy posts to servers operated by us.

  但是，当本应用访问第三方网站加载图片或搜索内容时，相关第三方网站可能会按照其自身隐私政策记录网络请求信息。

  However, when the App accesses third-party websites to load images or search content, those third parties may record network request information according to their own privacy policies.

  ## 5. 儿童隐私 / Children’s Privacy

  本应用不以儿童为主要目标用户，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

  The App is not primarily directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

  如果您认为儿童向本应用提供了个人信息，请通过下方联系方式联系我们。

  If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using the information below.

  ## 6. 数据安全 / Data Security

  本应用采取合理措施保护本地数据和应用运行数据。但由于互联网传输和电子存储无法保证绝对安全，我们无法保证数据传输或存储的绝对安全。

  We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored data and App-related information. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee
  absolute security.

  第三方网站的数据安全和隐私保护由相应网站负责，请参阅其隐私政策。

  The security and privacy practices of third-party websites are governed by their respective privacy policies.

  ## 7. 数据删除 / Data Deletion

  由于本应用不建立自有用户账号，也不在自有服务器上保存用户账号数据，因此不存在需要通过服务器账号注销流程删除的用户数据。

  Because the App does not operate user accounts or store account data on our own servers, there is no server-side account deletion process.

  您可以通过以下方式删除本地数据：

  - 在 Android 系统设置中清除本应用数据；
  - 卸载本应用；
  - 删除应用内支持删除的用户内容。

  You may delete locally stored data by:

  - Clearing the App’s data in Android settings;
  - Uninstalling the App;
  - Deleting user content that can be deleted within the App.

  ## 8. 隐私政策更新 / Changes to This Policy

  我们可能会根据应用功能变化、第三方服务变化或法律法规要求更新本隐私政策。

  We may update this Privacy Policy due to changes in App features, third-party services, or legal requirements.

  更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并显示新的生效日期。建议您定期查看本页面。

  The updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
